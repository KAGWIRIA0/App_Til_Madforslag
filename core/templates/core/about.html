The Story

Where DishCraft Came From

It's not a lack of food. It's not a lack of money. For most people, the daily struggle is simply not knowing what to cook and not having the energy to figure it out.

You open the fridge. There's plenty. But standing there trying to turn ingredients into a decision is more mentally draining than it should be. So people default to order something, repeat yesterday's meal, or skip a proper meal entirely. Not because they can't do better. Just because the decision itself is exhausting.

DishCraft was built around one idea: that decision should take seconds, not hours. Enter what you have. Enter what you can spend. Tell us what you've already eaten this week. And get clear, practical meal ideas instantly.

Whether you're a student working with a tight budget, cooking for a household, training for fitness, or just trying to eat something different tonight. DishCraft meets you where you are, without overcomplicating it.

The Full Picture

Six Sections. One App.

Each section is built around a specific part of how people eat, plan, and live.

01
Browse Dishes

Everything, All in One Place

Search and scroll through dishes by meal time. Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Anytime. Every dish comes with its full detail page: ingredients, add-ons, stew pairings, price, and a YouTube link. Browse freely or search by name, ingredient, or category.

02
Find My Dish

Search by What You Have

Enter your ingredients, your budget, or both. Add what you've eaten recently and we'll exclude it. Results come from the full dish library and Low Cost Meals so every budget and pantry situation is covered. There's also a Gemini AI tab for when you want a creative suggestion or can't find what you're after.

03
Low Cost Meals

Practical Meals for Tight Budgets

Built with students in mind, but genuinely useful for anyone watching their spending. Filter by budget, ingredients, or both. Stew options show their individual cost and whether they're within your budget. Includes a market price list and a Stock Up guide for items worth keeping around when money is available.

04
Gym & Performance

Food That Matches Your Training

Foods curated for fitness. Filtered by timing (pre-workout, during, post-workout), purpose (muscle gain, weight loss, endurance), and food category. Each entry includes macros, ingredients, a recipe, and a YouTube guide where available.

05
Skin & Body

Natural Results from Real Food

Expensive skincare products aren't always the answer. This section covers foods that genuinely support your skin for glow, hydration, anti-aging, and clearer skin. The Wellness tab covers immunity, gut health, sleep, energy, stress, and more.

06
Weekly Meal Planner

Plan Ahead, Remove the Daily Stress

Map out breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the whole week in advance. Click any saved meal to instantly pull it up in Browse, Find My Dish, or Low Cost Meals. Planning ahead means the "what do I eat today?" question is already answered.
